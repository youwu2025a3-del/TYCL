### 🧭 Built on Custom Content

**You are one of the Flameborn of Vaaz, a race created by S'kellak, the Creator of All Things, to protect and ensure the balance of all realms.**

**After thousands of years of peace, a ravenous force known as the Void has started an invasion into the universal realms. Worlds filled with life and death are now threatened with being destroyed completely.** **Will you fulfill your mission and stop the Void's Invasion?**

*Prominence is a modpack built on Custom Content, based on its lore universe.*

*Get ready for v4.0, the Kingdom of Vaaz Update, featuring for the first time in a Minecraft modpack a fully functional capital city filled with NPCs, which you will be able to visit at any point of the campaign, and which will evolve as you progress through the quests and eventually reach the Hasturian Era.*

<h3 align="center">⭐ Feature Overview ⭐</h3>

📜 <u>**Quests and Story Campaign**</u>
Guide yourself through the mods with interactive quests. Explore two major storylines: **The Void's Invasion** and **The Hasturian Era**.
The story campaign unlocks progressively, tying together **lore**, **narrative**, and your character's **progression**.

🌋 <u>**The Kingdom of Vaaz**</u>

A fully fledged **capital city**, home of the **Flameborn**, **Dwarven** and **Serkonid**.

🐍 Find various NPCs with dialogues, shops and quests.

⏳ Watch it evolve with your campaign progress and change over time.

🌲 <u>**The Talent Tree**</u>
Create your own class using a custom **Talent Tree** in Prominence's volcanic style.
It features **10 fates**, **16 passive talents**, **5 active abilities**, and **100 stat talents**.
Customize your build with unique talents and gear.

🔼 <u>**Level Up Your Character**</u>
Grow stronger with a custom leveling system.
Earn **talent points** every 2 levels and battle enemies that scale with your level.
Includes **multiplayer support** with mob scaling.

💬 <u>**NPC Dialogues**</u>

Two completely custom dialogue systems tied to NPCs:

🗣️ Immersive Dialogues, which contain **voice acted** (no AI used) lines and are used during campaign or important quests.
🔇 Interactive Dialogues, which use a custom screen to make players interact with the NPC, used by various NPCs across Vaaz.

🎨 <u>**Handcrafted Design**</u>
Prominence features a distinct **volcanic visual style** crafted by **S'Kellak**.
Includes a custom hotbar, custom GUI, changed textures, and a lot more immersive visual details throughout the modpack.

🔨 <u>**Forge Your Own Endgame**</u>
Choose your own playstyle with **powerful artifacts** featuring unique abilities.
Obtain the **Molten Core** through the campaign to forge artifacts.
Upgrade them through their own **Upgrade Tree** by gaining Artifact Power using **Molten Remnants**.

⚔️ <u>**The Primordial Forge: Endgame Custom Armor Sets**</u>

Forge endgame sets with unique tiered effects using the **Primordial Forge**, unlocked in Chapter II of the Hasturian Era.

💀 <u>**Mythic Challenges: Endgame Boss Encounters**</u>
Face off against **Mythic Bosses** with **infinite scaling** difficulty, which you need to defeat before the **timer runs out**.
Start at **Mythic 1** and fight increasingly harder bosses with random **affixes**.
⚠️ Up to 3 lives during multiplayer encounters; losing them makes the **Unholy Hourglass** level be reduced.
🔓 *Unlocked after completing Chapter II of the Hasturian Era.*

🧥 <u>**Transmogrification – Choose Your Own Style**</u>
Customize the appearance of your **armor** and **weapons** using magical **Transmogrification**.
Change any item's look without affecting its stats; **style freely without sacrificing power**!
